01 — ARTIST STATEMENT

Anna Wierciak aka Big Mama Dragon explores the meeting point between image, symbol, movement & transformation.

Anna Wierciak is a multidisciplinary artist whose work explores the universe, the subconscious, and alchemy through structural forms and surrealist occultism. Her practice brings together geometry, symbolism, intuitive mark-making, and organic forms to investigate the relationship between the visible and invisible, the material and psychological. Through painting and other visual practices, she translates internal states, esoteric concepts, and systems of transformation into complex symbolic structures. Her work explores how perception can shift when rational structures encounter intuition, symbolism, and the unknown.

Wierciak studied at the Liceum Plastyczne im. Józefa Szermentowskiego in Kielce, Poland, developing a foundation in visual art and traditional artistic practice. She later completed an interdisciplinary Bachelor’s degree in Humanities at Maastricht University in the Netherlands. This combination of formal artistic training and interdisciplinary humanities education informs her approach, which moves between visual language, philosophy, psychology, symbolism, and esoteric traditions. Her practice is ultimately concerned with transformation: how inner experience can become form, and how visual structures can open pathways into less visible dimensions of experience.

The work is an ongoing practice of translating experience into image, gesture, pattern & movement.
